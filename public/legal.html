Privacy

Waypoint doesn't collect anything. There's no account, no sign-up, and no form that sends your information anywhere.

Everything you enter — your team number, auto name, robot dimensions, and every step of your autonomous routine — is saved only in your browser's local storage, on your device. It's never transmitted to us or to any server; there's no backend at all. Clearing your browser's site data for this page deletes it permanently, and we have no copy to recover.

Waypoint is a static site hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel's infrastructure processes standard request data (such as IP address and timestamp) to serve the page and keep it running — we don't have access to this beyond normal hosting operation, and it's covered by Vercel's own privacy policy. No analytics or tracking scripts are added by the app itself.
